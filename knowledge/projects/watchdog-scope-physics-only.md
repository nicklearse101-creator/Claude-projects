Nik, 2026-09-28: "short cuts dont need to be recorded. we want to catch
manipulation of physics, nothing else. If we're not looking for physics
manipulation as a way of gaining an advantage, then there's no point in the
project." Said after I traced race 2's (24 Sep) sector-2 lap-time standout
to a corner cut and offered a shortcut detector plus counting lap-time
standouts in the flag tally.

**Why:** the whole point of [[ac-physics-watchdog-project]] is catching a
car whose physics has been tampered with (power, grip, speed, position).
Track cutting is stewarding, not the tool's job; proposing it reads as
drifting from the purpose.

**How to apply:** don't propose or build shortcut, track-limit or
driving-line features, and keep lap-time standouts out of the tally. When
the lap-time check flags something, judge it only on the physics in the
capture (speed, acceleration, cornering against the field and the car's
own other laps); if the gain came from something non-physical, say so in a
line and move on. Frame new ideas around catching physics manipulation,
e.g. the cross-night car fingerprint.
