## Engine (6)
The installed engine is a very detailed inline-four (twin-cam head, sidedraft intake, ~80k faces in the bay with baked
textures). No clean donor V8: Kunos' Lotus 49/72D DFV is merged into whole-car meshes and its trumpets are too tall for
the Escort's flat bonnet; the GT40's 289 is mid-engined with stacks too tall as well.
Plan: build a compact 90 deg race V8 to the physics' spec in Blender - block, DOHC heads with ribbed cam covers, low
plenum with 8 throttle bodies under an airbox (fits under the bonnet and the strut brace), fuel rail + injectors,
coils and leads, front timing cover, water pump, alternator, pulleys and belt, dry-sump pump + braided lines, 4-into-1
headers per bank dropping into the existing exhaust, using the car's own engine-bay materials.
**Honest confidence: medium** - the geometry can match the bay's level of detail, but the original's photographic
texture detail (castings, stampings, grime) can't be reproduced procedurally, so up close it will look cleaner.
Backup: the original engine stays in the blend (hidden, not exported - one call swaps it back) and the BACKUP v24 car
keeps the old engine installed.
