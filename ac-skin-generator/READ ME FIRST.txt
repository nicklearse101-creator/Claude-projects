RATTY'S4SKIN GENERATOR  v1.9.0
Makes liveries (skins) for Assetto Corsa cars. No AI, no internet needed.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Windows 10 or 11
- Assetto Corsa installed (Steam). Content Manager is handy but not required.
- Nothing else. Python is NOT needed.

INSTALL
1. Run "Rattys4SkinGenerator_Setup.exe".
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is not signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Choose where it goes: press "Change..." to pick a folder (for example on your D: drive).
   The app always gets its own Rattys4SkinGenerator folder in the one you pick.
   Tick "Keep my logos and settings in that folder too" to keep them there as well
   (then nothing of the app is kept on C:).
4. Click Install. No admin rights needed. You get a Desktop and Start-menu shortcut.
To update: run the new Setup - it offers the folder the app is in now. Your logos and
settings are never deleted by an update or by removing the app.
To remove it later: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Ratty's4Skin Generator.

MAKE A SKIN (2 minutes)
1. CAR tab: the Assetto Corsa cars folder is found automatically. Type part of the
   car's name and pick it. Give the skin a name.
   Tick "Sharper textures (4K)" for crisper writing (bigger files).
   "Smaller files" (on by default) makes the skin about a quarter of the size.
2. LIVERY tab: pick the base colour, accent colours and a pattern (a small picture shows it).
3. NUMBER & NAMES tab: racing number, team name, driver name, a font for each (you see it as you pick),
   and tick where each one is written on the car.
4. LOGOS tab: about 230 logos are already in the library. "Add logos..." to add your own
   pictures (PNG with a clear background is best). Select a logo and choose its place on the car.
   You can put several logos on the same place: they start side by side and you move each
   one on the preview. The "On the car" list shows which logos are on and where;
   "Clear all" takes them all off (they stay in the library). Tick "Keep logos off areas
   the car mirrors" if you never want a backwards copy (some cars use one picture for both
   halves of the bonnet / roof).
5. WINDOWS tab (optional): tick "Make new window stickers" for a windscreen banner
   and number / name on the windows (move, resize or delete them on the preview).
   Tick "Tint the windows" for dark glass: pick the colour and how dark; the windscreen
   is only tinted if you tick that too.
   Some cars use one glass picture for several windows. There the app adds the sticker
   through the skin's ext_config.ini (needs Custom Shaders Patch; no car files are changed,
   so nothing changes online). Other drivers see it if they have the skin and CSP.
6. LIGHTS & WHEELS tab (optional): headlight lens colour (bright colours only), rim colour and tyre colour
   (rims and tyres are separate).
7. Press PREVIEW. Wait for the bar to finish (up to about a minute the first time for a car).
8. Happy? Press CREATE SKIN IN CAR. Straight after a preview this only takes a few seconds
   (it uses the preview's work, as long as nothing was changed in between).
9. In Content Manager pick the car, pick the new skin and click "Update preview".
   (Without Content Manager: the skin is simply there in the game's car menu.)

MOVE THINGS ABOUT ON THE PREVIEW
1. After a preview, pick ONE side at the top: Left, Right, Front, Rear or Top.
2. Every logo, number, name and window sticker has a dashed box. Click one to pick it.
3. Drag = move.  Mouse wheel = bigger / smaller.  Shift + wheel = turn.
   Or use the buttons: Bigger, Smaller, Turn, Flip (mirror), Edit text..., Delete, Reset.
   Edit text... changes the words, font and colour of that one team / driver name.
   Delete takes it off the car ("Reset all" brings everything back).
4. Press PREVIEW again to redraw the car with your changes (much quicker the second time).
Moves apply to both sides of the car. Changes are remembered for each car.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Logos, numbers and names go exactly where you asked. They are never moved, shrunk or
  left off because they overlap each other - move them on the preview if you like.
- The box under the buttons (the log) tells you what the app did, e.g. if a logo hung
  off its panel and was slid onto it.
- Some cars use one picture for both halves of a panel (for example the Kadett bonnet).
  There a logo also shows mirrored on the other half - that is how the car was made.
  Move the logo on the preview if you don't like it.
- Making a skin with a name you already used asks before replacing it. Skins the
  app did not make are never overwritten.
- Some cars cannot take everything:
    * a few cars only allow a plain colour (their paint has no room for a design);
    * some cars have no room on the glass for window stickers;
    * wheels and headlights only change on cars that have their own texture for them;
    * cars locked (encrypted) by their maker cannot be read at all.
  The log says so when this happens.
- Your settings and logo library are kept in the app's folder ("data") if you ticked
  "Keep my logos and settings in that folder too" when installing, otherwise in
  %APPDATA%\Rattys4SkinGenerator. Either way they survive an update and an uninstall.

PROBLEMS
- "Pick a car first": choose the car on the Car tab.
- The cars folder is empty: on the Car tab press "..." and choose
  ...\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\content\cars
- Skin looks wrong in the game: press "Update preview" in Content Manager, or
  restart the game.
