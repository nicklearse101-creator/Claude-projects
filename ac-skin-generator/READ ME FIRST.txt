  RATTY'S4SKIN GENERATOR  v1.17.2          -  the user guide
  Custom liveries (skins) for Assetto Corsa cars. No AI, no internet, no Photoshop.
=====================================================================================

IN A HURRY? (the 60-second version)
  1. Run Rattys4SkinGenerator_Setup.exe and click Install.
  2. Open the app. A short tour shows you round - follow it once.
  3. Car tab: pick your car and give the skin a name.
  4. Livery tab: pick colours and a pattern.
  5. Number & names tab: your number, team and name.
  6. Press PREVIEW. Drag logos and names about on the car if you like.
  7. Press CREATE SKIN IN CAR. In Content Manager pick the skin, click "Update preview". Race.
  Rest the mouse on anything in the app for a tip.

1. WHAT YOU NEED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Windows 10 or 11.
  - Assetto Corsa (Steam). Content Manager is handy but not needed.
  - Custom Shaders Patch is only needed for a few extras (section 12).
  - Nothing else. Python is NOT needed. No internet needed.


2. INSTALLING, UPDATING, REMOVING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Install
  1. Run "Rattys4SkinGenerator_Setup.exe".
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (the app is not signed).
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Choose where it goes: "Change..." picks a folder (for example on your D: drive).
     The app always gets its own Rattys4SkinGenerator folder inside the one you pick.
     Tick "Keep my logos and settings in that folder too" to keep everything there
     (then nothing of the app lives on C:).
  4. Click Install. No admin rights needed. You get a Desktop and a Start-menu shortcut.

  Update: run the new Setup. It offers the folder the app is in now. Your logos and
  settings are never deleted by an update.

  Remove: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Ratty's4Skin Generator.
  Your logos and settings are kept (see section 16) in case you come back.


3. THE APP AT A GLANCE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Left:   tabs - Car, Livery, Number & names, Logos, Windows, Wheels.
          Under them: PREVIEW, CREATE SKIN IN CAR, the progress bar and a message box
          (the "log") that says what the app did.
  Right:  the preview - your car in a garage, with buttons above it to pick a side and
          to edit what is on the car.

  Every time the app opens it starts clean: no car picked and a blank livery (a plain
  white car). Your logo library, your cars folder and your app settings are kept.

  Help
  - The first time the app opens, a short tour shows what each part does.
  - Rest the mouse on any control for a tip.
  - Car tab: "Show me round" starts the tour again. Untick "Show help" when you know your
    way round (no more tips or tour).

  Drop-down lists: click to open them, or use the Up / Down arrow keys or the mouse
  wheel over them to step through the choices without opening them.


4. CAR TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Start new livery     Clears the livery back to a plain white car at any time: colours,
                       pattern, number, names, logos on the car, window stickers, tint,
                       wheels and hand moves. Your car and logo library stay.
  Show help / Show me round   See section 3.
  AC cars folder       Found by itself (through Steam). Only change it if your game is
                       somewhere unusual: "..." and choose
                       ...\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\content\cars
  Car                  The car to make a skin for. Type part of its name to find it.
  Start from           Change a skin that is already on the car - pick it and it shows on the
                       preview by itself:
                       - a skin made with this app (1.12 or later) comes back exactly as you
                         made it: colours, pattern, writing, logos and your hand moves. Saving
                         replaces it (it asks first);
                       - any other skin (the car's own, a downloaded one, or one made with an
                         older version) keeps its paint, and your number, names and logos go on
                         top. It is saved as a NEW skin ("<name> custom") - the original is never
                         changed.
  Skin name            The name of the new skin. Using a name again replaces a skin you
                       made before with this app (it asks first). It never overwrites a
                       skin the app did not make - a stock skin is always safe.
  Windows etc. from    Optional. Copies the car's other files (windows, interior,
                       number plates...) from one of the car's own skins.
  App colours          The app's own look: Carbon Orange, Ratty Purple, Ice Blue,
                       Racing Green, Red Line, Stealth Grey, Light. The app restarts in the
                       new colours and keeps your livery.
  Report this car...   See section 15.
  Sharper textures (4K)  Crisper writing and logos; bigger files and a slower build.
  Smaller files        Compressed textures - about a quarter of the size, look the same
                       in the game. Leave it on.


5. LIVERY TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Colours
    Base colour        The main paint.
    Accent colour      The pattern's main colour.
    Highlight colour   Thin stripes and edges in the pattern (and logo glows).
    Text colour        The number and names.

  Pattern
    50 designs, from classic stripes, hockey sticks and chequered fades to swooshes,
    speed lines, camo, flames, circuit boards and GT sashes. The little car under the
    list shows the pattern on its side and top in your colours.
    "Gallery..." shows every pattern at once - click one to use it.
    Every pattern wraps round the car in 3D, so it lines up across panels and reads the
    right way on both sides.

  Pattern from a picture
    Choose "Pattern from a picture..." in the list, then a picture:
    - a side view of a car (front on the left or on the right) - fitted to your car, or
    - a flat pattern / wrap print.
    Simplify (Off / Light / Medium / Strong) melts small writing and stickers in the
    picture away. "Use my colours instead of the picture's" repaints it in your colours.
    Your number, names and logos go on top as usual.

  Halftone dots        Comic-style dots up the lower sides of the car, big at the bottom
                       and fading out upwards. They sit on the plain paint, not over the
                       pattern.
  Paint finish         Car's own / Gloss / Metallic / Pearlescent / Matte. Needs Custom
                       Shaders Patch in the game (section 12). The app's preview can't show
                       shine - look in the game.


6. NUMBER & NAMES TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Racing number        Leave it empty for no number.
  Number style         Number board (on a white board), Plain number, or No number.
  Show number on       Doors and / or roof.
  Team / wordmark      Your team name (or any words).
  Driver name          Your name.
  Team / Driver / Number font
                       Pick a font for each - the preview under it shows your words.
                       The "Racing:" fonts at the top come with the app (Russo One,
                       Orbitron, Exo 2, Bebas Neue, Titillium, Faster One and more), so
                       everyone has them. Below them are the fonts installed on your PC.
  Where to write them  Tick where the team name and the driver name go: front wings,
                       lower doors, doors, rear quarters, bonnet, roof, boot, rear, front.
                       Where both are ticked, the name goes under the team.

  Writing always gets a readable colour and a thin outline, so it shows over any pattern.


7. LOGOS TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Your logo library. About 230 logos come with the app; add your own with
  "Add logos..." (PNG with a see-through background works best). They stay in the
  library for next time.

  Put a logo on the car
    1. Click the logo in the list.
    2. Pick a place: Doors, Bonnet, Front wings, Rear quarters, Lower doors (sill), Roof,
       Boot lid, Rear panel, Front bumper, Windscreen banner, Rear window or Rear side
       windows.
    3. Pick more places to use the same logo on several parts (e.g. bonnet AND rear
       quarters).
  "On the car" lists every logo on the car and where. "Take off" removes the one picked
  there; choosing "Not used" takes a logo off everywhere. "Clear all" takes every logo
  off the car (they stay in the library). "Remove" deletes a logo from the library.

  Background           Auto removes a plain background round a logo and keeps busy
                       pictures whole; or choose Remove / Keep whole picture yourself.
  Tint glow            A soft glow round the logo in your highlight colour.
  Keep logos off areas the car mirrors
                       Some cars use one half of a picture for both sides of a panel, so
                       a logo there shows backwards on the other half. Tick to avoid it.

  Several logos can share one place: they start side by side and you move each one on
  the preview. Once the car has been previewed, anything already on it stays where it
  is: a logo added later is fitted round the others (slid along or up/down its panel,
  made smaller if it has to be).


8. WINDOWS TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Window stickers
    Make new window stickers   A banner across the top of the windscreen and rear window
                               (full width) plus your number / name on the windows.
    Remove the stickers already on the windows   Clears the car's own stickers first.
    Banner text / colour / height (8-25 cm) / sticker text colour.
    Also show                  Racing number and / or driver name on the rear window and
                               the side windows chosen in "Side stickers on" (rear side
                               windows, front door windows or both).
    Inside the car the stickers show their dark back, like real ones.

  Tint
    Tint the windows           Tinted glass like a window film: pick the colour and how
                               dark (5-90 %). The windscreen stays clear unless you tick
                               "Tint the windscreen too". Stickers sit on top of the tint.

  Some cars use one glass picture for several windows. There the app adds the stickers
  and tint through the skin's own CSP settings (section 12).


9. WHEELS TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Recolour the wheel rims and / or the tyres (separate colours). Only cars whose rims or
  tyres have their own texture can change.


9b. INTERIOR TAB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Seats & leather, Alcantara / suede, Fabric / cloth, Roll cage, Carpet / floor,
  Dashboard, Seat belts: tick a part and pick its colour. The material stays - grain,
  stitching and shading are kept - only the colour changes.
  - The app finds the parts by their names in the car; the log says which it changed
    and which it left alone.
  - A part whose texture the outside of the car also uses is left alone (on some cars
    the dashboard plastic is the same as the outside trim), and so are displays, glass,
    carbon and metal.
  - The preview shows the outside of the car only: look inside in the game (cockpit
    view or close up).
  - Not on protected cars.


10. PREVIEW, AND MOVING THINGS ON THE CAR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PREVIEW shows your livery on the car from every side WITHOUT saving anything.
  The first preview of a car takes up to about a minute (the app studies the car);
  after that it is quick. The app starts studying a car as soon as you pick it, while
  you set up the livery, so the first preview is quicker if you don't press Preview
  straight away. Logos, numbers and names you have not moved are not worked out again.
  The light and shade on your paint comes from the shape of the car itself (like the
  ambient-occlusion maps Content Manager bakes), worked out once per car - nothing of the
  car's old skins shows under your livery, not even between the panels. Trim that every
  stock skin keeps the same (arch flares, sills, pillar edges, mirrors) takes your livery
  too; grilles, intakes, lights and mirror glass stay as they are.

  Above the picture
    All sides / Left / Right / Front / Rear / Top   which view to show.
    Grid           Faint lines every 25 cm and an orange centre line, to line things up.
    Open full size Opens the view as a picture.

  Move things on the car
    1. Pick ONE side (Left, Right, Front, Rear or Top).
    2. Every logo, number, name and window sticker has a dashed box. Click one to pick it.
    3. Drag it - the logo itself moves on the car as you drag.
       Mouse wheel = bigger / smaller.   Shift + mouse wheel = turn.
       Or the buttons: Bigger, Smaller, Turn, Flip (mirror), Edit text... (words, font
       and colour of that one name), Delete, Reset (that one), Reset all.
    4. Let go and the car redraws itself after a few seconds - no need to press Preview.
       You can keep moving things while it redraws.
  Moves apply to both sides of the car (left and right match).
  What is on the car stays put: after a preview every logo, number and name keeps its
  spot, so adding another logo or a name later only places the new one (round the
  others). Reset (that one) lets the app place it again; Reset all lays out everything
  afresh.

  "NOT PLACED"
    If the app can't find a spot for something on a car (for example the bonnet of an odd
    model), it is not quietly left off - a NOT PLACED bar shows above the picture.
    Pick one side (for a bonnet logo: Top), choose the item, press "Pick it on the car"
    and click where it should go. The car redraws itself with it there.


11. SAVING THE SKIN INTO THE GAME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  CREATE SKIN IN CAR saves the skin into the car's skins folder. Straight after a
  preview it only takes a few seconds.
  - Same name as a skin you made before: it asks, then replaces it (the old one is only
    removed once the new one is built).
  - A skin the app did not make (stock or downloaded) is NEVER overwritten - choose
    another name.
  Then: in Content Manager pick the car, pick your skin and click "Update preview".
  Without Content Manager the skin is simply there in the game's car menu.
  Online: other drivers see your skin if they have the skin folder too.


12. THINGS THAT NEED CUSTOM SHADERS PATCH (CSP)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Most of a skin works in plain Assetto Corsa. These extras need CSP (most drivers have
  it) and work through the skin's own settings file (ext_config.ini) - no car file is
  ever changed, so nothing changes for online checksums:
  - Paint finish (gloss, metallic flakes, pearl, matte).
  - Window stickers / tint on cars that use one glass picture for several windows.
  Other drivers see these if they have CSP and your skin.


13. WHAT SOME CARS CAN'T DO
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Protected cars: some mods are protected by their makers with CSP (many GT4,
    Supercars and some BTCC / GT3 mods). The app can't see how their paint wraps round
    the car, so it paints the car's flat paint sheet instead (2D MODE), the way you would
    in GIMP: it starts from the maker's template skin (or the car's lightest skin), keeps
    its shading and its carbon parts, and puts your colours and pattern on it.
      * The preview shows the sheet, not the car - check the result in the game.
      * Drag the number, names and logos on the sheet; the copy on the other side of the
        car follows (and reads the right way there).
      * "Front of the car" (above the sheet): which way the car's front points on the roof /
        bonnet panel, so logos there face the right way.
      * Camo-style patterns look best; striped ones may run different ways on different
        panels. Wheels, window stickers and tint stay as the car has them.
    If a protected car has no skin to paint over, only a plain colour is possible. The app
    never tries to get round the protection.
  - A few cars only allow a plain colour (their paint picture has no room for a design).
  - Some cars have no room on the glass for window stickers.
  - Wheels only change on cars that have their own wheel / tyre texture.
  - Cars locked (encrypted) completely by their maker can't be read at all.
  The log under the buttons says so whenever this happens.


14. GOOD TO KNOW
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Logos, numbers and names go exactly where you asked: never moved, shrunk or left off
    because they overlap - move them on the preview if you like. Only something added
    after a preview is fitted round what is already on the car; if there is no room the
    log says so and you move it.
  - The log tells you what the app did, e.g. if a logo hung off its panel and was slid
    onto it.
  - Some cars use one picture for both halves of a panel (for example the Kadett
    bonnet): a logo there also shows mirrored on the other half. That is how the car was
    made - move the logo, or tick "Keep logos off areas the car mirrors".
  - Typical skin size with "Smaller files" on: around 10-30 MB (more with 4K).
  - Your hand moves are kept while the app is open, until you start a new livery.


15. PROBLEMS? (and how to report a car)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - The app gets a car wrong: Car tab > "Report this car...". It saves a small zip on
    your desktop (what the app found in the car, its messages, your design and the
    preview - no game files). Post it on the Ratty's Racing Discord with a line about
    what went wrong.
  - "Pick a car first": choose the car on the Car tab.
  - The car list is empty: Car tab > "..." and choose
    ...\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\content\cars
  - The skin looks wrong or old in the game: click "Update preview" in Content Manager,
    or restart the game.
  - Paint finish / some window stickers don't show: they need Custom Shaders Patch.
  - Windows "protected your PC" when installing: "More info" > "Run anyway".


16. YOUR FILES AND SETTINGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Your logo library and settings live in the app's "data" folder (if you ticked "Keep
    my logos and settings in that folder too" when installing), otherwise in
    %APPDATA%\Rattys4SkinGenerator. Updates and removing the app never delete them.
  - Skins go into ...\assettocorsa\content\cars\<car>\skins\<your skin name>.
  - The app keeps a little scratch folder next to your cars (assettocorsa\_r4skin_work).
