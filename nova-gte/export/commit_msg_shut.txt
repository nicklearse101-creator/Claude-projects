Character-line network v1: 28 key lines as fitted B-splines in FreeCAD

- tools/cad/lines.py: the Nova's key lines assembled from the traced drawings,
  GA plan and photo measurements (blender/data/cad/lines_v1.json).
- tools/cad/build_curves.py: least-squares cubic B-splines within one source
  pixel, split at real corners (40 mm chords); spans capped so the fit can no
  longer go singular (the 12:22 run hung on that). 24 of 28 within tolerance.
  -> cad/nova_lines.FCStd, lines_v1_fit.json
- tools/cad/check_lines.py: draws the lines on every drawing view and photo.
- tools/fetch_commons.ps1: free Commons photos of pre-facelift 3-doors.
