1989 Vauxhall Nova GTE — Engine & Engine Bay Reference Pack
============================================================

Target: standard UK right-hand-drive 1989 Vauxhall Nova GTE, no aftermarket modifications.

ENGINE IDENTIFICATION
---------------------
The Nova GTE used the 1.6-litre E16SE petrol engine: inline four-cylinder, single overhead camshaft,
8 valves, fuel injection, front-mounted and driving the front wheels through a manual gearbox.
Period and auction sources commonly quote about 99–100 bhp. Confirm exact details against a factory
workshop manual and the specific car being modelled.

WHAT TO MODEL
-------------
- E16SE 1.6-litre SOHC 8-valve engine and cylinder head
- Intake manifold / throttle-body and injection components
- Exhaust manifold on the opposite side of the cylinder head
- Distributor / ignition leads and period air-intake plumbing
- Alternator, pulleys, belts and brackets
- Radiator, cooling hoses, expansion tank and washer bottle
- Battery, fuse/relay/electrical boxes, wiring looms and earth straps
- Engine mounts, front crossmember / subframe and gearbox
- Brake servo and master cylinder at the bulkhead; right-hand-drive brake and steering packaging
- Inner wings, slam panel, bonnet underside and factory-looking fasteners/labels

STOCK-TRIM CAUTIONS
-------------------
Auction photos can show replacement batteries, hoses, clamps, leads or other service items. Do not assume
every visible component is factory-original. Avoid aftermarket cone filters, polished parts, custom
manifolds, non-standard wiring, strut braces and performance add-ons unless the target car specifically has them.
A replacement battery or service hose may differ from original but is not necessarily a performance modification.

SCALING FOR BLENDER
-------------------
Use metric units. The overall Nova wheelbase is commonly listed at approximately 2,343 mm; this is a useful
provisional chassis reference, not a measurement of the engine itself. The overall length and width do not
establish engine-bay dimensions. The photo is perspective-distorted, so do not measure parts from pixels and
treat the result as exact.

Suggested workflow:
1. Block out front chassis rails, inner wings, bulkhead and slam panel from orthogonal views.
2. Place the engine and gearbox as separate objects and verify bonnet clearance.
3. Establish the crankshaft / gearbox axis and front-wheel drive layout before modelling detail.
4. Use close-up references or a workshop manual for individual parts; label photo-derived dimensions as estimates.
5. Keep the RHD brake servo/master-cylinder and steering-column arrangement on the correct side.
6. Save measured/estimated dimensions in a Blender notes object or a companion text file.
